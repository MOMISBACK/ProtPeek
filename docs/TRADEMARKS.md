ProtPeek is open-source software. Its source code can be used, studied,
modified, and redistributed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. That
licence does not grant rights to the names, service marks, or logos of its
contributors.

## Reserved brand assets

The **ProtPeek** name, word mark, logo, application icons, extension-listing
identity, and distinctive visual brand are reserved by MOMISBACK. They are
not licensed under the MPL-2.0. No permission is granted to use them as the
name or branding of a fork, modified build, competing product, service, or
browser-store listing.

The reserved logo and icon files are listed in [NOTICE](../NOTICE).

## Forks and modified distributions

You may fork and modify the code under the MPL-2.0. Before publishing or
redistributing a modified version, you must:

- choose a new product, extension, package, and repository name that is not
  confusingly similar to ProtPeek;
- replace the ProtPeek logo, icons, and other distinctive brand assets;
- change browser-extension identifiers and store-listing identity where
  applicable;
- state clearly that the project is unofficial and is not endorsed by or
  affiliated with ProtPeek or MOMISBACK.

In particular, there is **no authorisation to republish a fork under the
ProtPeek name or with the official ProtPeek logo**.

A GitHub source fork created solely to propose contributions may retain the
automatically generated repository name while it is clearly shown as a fork.
This narrow collaboration exception does not permit publishing a browser
extension, release, website, service, or store listing under the ProtPeek name.

Private development builds may retain the branding for testing. A bit-for-bit
identical official release downloaded from the official repository may retain
the branding when redistributed unchanged and with its source/version clearly
identified. Rebuilding or repackaging creates a modified distribution and is
not covered by this exception.

## Permitted references

Truthful, non-misleading references such as “based on the ProtPeek source
code” and links to the official repository are permitted when reasonably
necessary to describe origin or compatibility. This does not permit use of
ProtPeek as the fork's own name or branding, nor does it limit rights that
cannot lawfully be restricted.

## Contributions

Submitting an issue or pull request does not grant any trademark right.
Only code explicitly reviewed and merged by the project maintainer becomes
part of an official ProtPeek release.

Questions or requests for written brand permission can be opened at
<https://github.com/MOMISBACK/ProtPeek/issues>.

This policy describes the project's intended brand permissions and is not
legal advice.
